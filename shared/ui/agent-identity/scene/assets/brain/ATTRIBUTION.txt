Cortical surface asset

BrainMesh_ICBM152.lh.mz3, distributed by NiiVue.
Source: https://github.com/niivue/niivue/blob/main/packages/niivue/demos/images/BrainMesh_ICBM152.lh.mz3
Retained unmodified as cortex-left.mz3. The demo mirrors the left hemisphere,
centres/scales the geometry, and uses custom materials. This is a stylised UI
illustration, not an anatomical or clinical visualisation.
